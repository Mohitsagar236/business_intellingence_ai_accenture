§1

Introduction

1.1Purpose

This Software Requirements Specification defines the functional and non-functional requirements for BusinessIntelligence.ai, a KPI storytelling and root-cause diagnosis engine. It is written for the development team, evaluators, and stakeholders of the Accenture Innovation Challenge 2026, and is intended to be precise enough to design and test against.

1.2Scope

The system ingests structured and unstructured data from department systems, isolates statistically significant KPI anomalies from normal variation, determines the most probable root cause by requiring structured and unstructured evidence to converge, and produces a grounded, department-ready diagnosis and recommended action. Where the evidence does not converge, the system reports the ambiguity honestly rather than fabricating confidence.

Out of scope for v1: autonomous execution of recommended actions, sub-minute real-time alerting, and data sources beyond CRM, ERP, ticketing, finance, and HR systems.

§2

Overall Description

2.1Product Perspective

BusinessIntelligence.ai is a new analytical layer sitting between an organization's systems of record and the analysts who currently perform root-cause analysis by hand. It reads from those systems and writes a structured report back to the responsible department — it does not replace or modify the source systems.

2.2Product Functions

  • Ingest structured and unstructured data on a scheduled or triggered basis
  • Filter statistical noise from genuine anomalies
  • Segment anomalies and mine structured and unstructured evidence in parallel
  • Validate convergence of evidence before naming a cause
  • Handle ambiguous cases transparently, with ranked hypotheses
  • Match validated causes to playbooks and generate a grounded report
  • Route the finished report to the responsible department

2.3User Classes & Characteristics

User classNeeds
Business AnalystEvidence and confidence behind a finding, not just a verdict
Department Head / Ops LeadAn action, delivered to the right channel, not a data dump
Data / IT AdministratorControl over source connectors, access, and the suppressed-report log
Executive SponsorPeriodic aggregate view of what fired, what didn't, and outcomes

2.4Operating Environment

Cloud-hosted service with a browser-based reporting UI. Integration with client systems via API, webhook, or replicated read access. Report delivery via email, Slack, Teams, or in-app dashboard.

2.5Design & Implementation Constraints

  • Every asserted cause must be traceable to specific evidence — a structured metric and an unstructured excerpt
  • No recommendation may be issued without passing both the significance check and the convergence check
  • LLM narrative generation is retrieval-grounded: it may phrase findings, never invent them

2.6Assumptions & Dependencies

  • Source systems expose queryable APIs or replicated read access
  • At least one full seasonal cycle of historical data (e.g. 12 months) is available for baseline modelling
  • Organizational playbooks exist, or can be authored, per cause category
§3

Functional Requirements

Each requirement carries a priority under MoSCoW: Must is required for v1, Should is expected but deferrable, Could is a stretch goal.

3.1Data Ingestion

FR-1.1Must

The system shall connect to structured data sources: CRM, ERP, ticketing, finance, and HR systems.

FR-1.2Must

The system shall ingest unstructured sources: call notes, tickets, surveys, and reports.

FR-1.3Should

The system should normalize and timestamp-align all sources to a common entity and time key.

FR-1.4Could

The system could support both scheduled and on-demand / triggered ingestion.

3.2Noise Filtering

FR-2.1Must

The system shall run data-quality checks — sync errors, duplicates, definition changes — before any analysis proceeds.

FR-2.2Must

The system shall decompose each monitored KPI's time series via STL into trend, seasonal, and residual components.

FR-2.3Must

The system shall apply sample-size-scaled confidence intervals to judge whether a residual deviation is statistically significant.

FR-2.4Must

The system shall suppress and silently log any non-significant deviation — no report is issued for it.

FR-2.5Should

The system should expose the suppressed-report log to administrators for trust auditing.

3.3Root-Cause Analysis

FR-3.1Must

The system shall segment a confirmed anomaly by region, product, account, and channel.

FR-3.2Must

The system shall correlate co-moving structured metrics within the anomaly's time window.

FR-3.3Must

The system shall mine unstructured text for themes via NLP clustering, scoped to the same window and segment.

FR-3.4Must

The system shall run a convergence check requiring structured and unstructured evidence to align in both timing and direction before naming a single cause.

FR-3.5Must

Where no single cause converges, the system shall invoke the Ambiguous Case Handler rather than assert a cause.

FR-3.6Must

The Ambiguous Case Handler shall present ranked hypotheses with relative confidence, and name the specific evidence gap that would disambiguate each.

3.4Recommendation & Reporting

FR-4.1Must

The system shall map a validated root cause to a predefined, vetted playbook.

FR-4.2Must

The system shall generate a natural-language report — Problem, Cause, Confidence, Action — strictly grounded in validated evidence.

FR-4.3Must

The system shall flag or reject any generated statement that is not traceable to retrieved evidence (anti-hallucination guard).

FR-4.4Should

The system should automatically route the finished report to the owning department's channel.

FR-4.5Could

The system could let analysts annotate a report to improve future playbook matching.

§4

External Interface Requirements

4.1User Interfaces

A web dashboard for report review; an admin console for source connectors and the suppressed-report audit log; a no-code playbook editor.

4.2Software Interfaces

CRM (e.g. Salesforce), ERP, ticketing (e.g. Zendesk, ServiceNow), finance systems, HRIS, an LLM inference API, and an NLP / embedding service.

4.3Communication Interfaces

Inbound REST and webhook APIs; outbound email, Slack, and Teams delivery; HTTPS/TLS throughout.

§5

Non-Functional Requirements

IDRequirement
NFR-1Performance — end-to-end diagnosis completes within minutes per incident, not days.
NFR-2Trustworthiness — the system never asserts a cause without meeting the convergence threshold; genuine ambiguity is surfaced, never hidden.
NFR-3Security & privacy — encryption in transit and at rest; role-based access to connectors and reports; PII-aware handling of call-note and survey text.
NFR-4Scalability — concurrent anomaly evaluation across many KPIs and segments without cross-contamination.
NFR-5Usability — reports are readable by a non-technical department lead: Problem, Cause, Confidence, Action, in that order.
NFR-6Auditability — every report retains a link to its underlying structured and unstructured evidence.
§6

Other Requirements

  • Compliance with client data-residency and retention policy
  • Suppressed-log data retained for a configurable audit window
  • Playbooks are version-controlled with a visible change history
Appendix A

Representative Use Cases

UC-1

Regional revenue drop, routed to Sales Ops

A regional revenue KPI breaches its confidence interval. Segmentation isolates the region; correlated billing-system errors and a spike in “payment failed” ticket themes converge in timing. A grounded report is generated and routed to Sales Ops within minutes.

UC-2

Ambiguous CSAT decline

Support CSAT drops significantly, but staffing-shortfall and product-regression hypotheses both partially fit. The Ambiguous Case Handler presents both, ranked, and names the missing agent-headcount log that would resolve the ambiguity.

UC-3

Data-quality issue silently suppressed

A metric dip traces back to a finance-system definition change rather than a real event. The system suppresses the report entirely and logs the cause silently, preserving trust in future alerts.
